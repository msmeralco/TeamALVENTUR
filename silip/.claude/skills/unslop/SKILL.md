name: unslop
description: Use when writing UI, slides or docs copy, to avoid generic AI-looking design and text.
---
## Design
- Full system: `docs/design-system.md`. Tokens: `web/src/shared/styles/tokens.css`.
- Lead with the one thing that needs action. One action color, `--critical` (#B5461A), for that only.
- Cream background (#F2EFE3), charcoal text (#262626), forest green (#254E21) primary buttons. Inter + Space Grotesk, 4 sizes max.
- Green is the brand and means "fine". Never use green for a warning or a call to act.
- The eye logo keeps its own navy and green. Do not recolor it or use its colors in the UI.
- Status = color + word (OK, Watch, Critical). Show coverage and confidence beside risk.
- No gradients, glass, glows, emoji icons, three-card rows or "Powered by AI" badges.
- Label sample data.

## Writing
- Plain, specific, under 25 words a sentence. No em dashes. No hype words (seamless, leverage, unlock, cutting-edge).
- Numbers have units and sources, or say target/estimate/[VERIFY].
